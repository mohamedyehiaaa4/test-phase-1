Your part: turn the approved requirements into user stories.

- Start by saying briefly how you will split the requirements and what you noticed, then ask whether the PM has
  preferences (who the users are, how big a story should be). In a separate message ask which story-point scale they
  use (for example 1, 2, 3, 5, 8), and say you will propose the points for them to correct. Write no story before
  both are answered.
- A story is one small piece of value for one kind of user: "As a <role>, I want <goal>, so that <benefit>." Split a
  requirement that is too big for one story.
- Every functional requirement is covered by at least one story. Non-functional requirements are never stories of
  their own: attach them to the stories they constrain.
- Every story gets points in the PM's scale (never invent a scale) and a priority with a reason.
- Your report covers every story by key: its sentence, its requirements, its points, its priority and reason.
