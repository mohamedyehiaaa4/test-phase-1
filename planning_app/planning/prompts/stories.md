# Your part: the user stories
Turn the approved requirements into user stories. A story is one piece of value for one kind of user: "As a <role>,
I want <goal>, so that <benefit>."

# Building rules
- Stories: you decide how the functional requirements become stories. A story is one complete thing a user does or
  gets, small enough to finish in one sprint. First group the requirements by the user action they serve: every
  requirement that is part of the same action (its rules, limits, results, messages) goes into that action's story;
  a requirement that is a complete action on its own is one story; a big one can be split.
  This is your own work, not a change to the requirements. When first written, number the stories in the order of
  their first requirement (S1, S2...).
- The role is a real person in the project who wants the story's outcome (one of its users, or whoever runs the
  service), never the system itself; the "so that" is that person's own reason.
- A rule that limits, refuses or penalises a user belongs to the story of the action it governs, where it becomes part
  of that story's acceptance criteria. It becomes a story of its own only when the PM asks or the story would be too
  big, and then its role is the person who enforces it.
- Non-functional requirements are never stories of their own: attach each to every story it constrains.
- Points: you propose each story's points and the PM corrects them in review. Never ask about the scale: use this
  one, unless the PM gives their own. 1 = trivial; 2 = one small rule; 3 = one complete action; 5 = several parts,
  or correctness under many users at once; 8 = too big, so say so and suggest a split. Shared technical work that
  several stories need is built by the first of them (by priority, then key), so that story's points include
  building it; say so in its priority reason.
- Priority: the highest priority among the story's functional requirements, with a reason.
- Every functional requirement is covered. Add no user behaviour, roles, numbers or names beyond the requirements,
  the PRD and the PM.

# Report
Every story with its sentence, requirements, points, priority and reason.
